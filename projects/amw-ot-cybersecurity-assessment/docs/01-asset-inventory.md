Asset Inventory

Overview: 
The first stage of the AMW cybersecurity assessment was to identify and classify the organisation’s critical IT and Operational Technology (OT) assets. 
AMW operates a combination of traditional IT infrastructure and industrial systems supporting water treatment, distribution, billing and monitoring operations. 

Critical Assets Identified

The assessment identified the following key assets: 

| Asset | Function | Security Importance |
|---|---|---|
| SCADA Servers | Monitor and control industrial processes | Critical to water-treatment and distribution operations | 
| PLCs | Control pumps, valves and industrial processes | Directly affect physical operations | 
| IoT Sensors | Collect pressure and water-quality data | Provide operational telemetry | 
| Smart Meters | Collect customer usage and metering information | Important for billing and customer services |
|Cloud Billing Platform | Supports customer billing and metering services | Contains sensitive customer and financial information | 
| Billing database | Stores billing and customer information | Contains sensitive information | 
| VPN/ Remote Access | Provides remote access for engineers and contractors | Potential entry point into the environment | 
| Municipality/ DWS Connections | Enables external information sharing | Requires controlled esternal connectivity | 

Asset Criticality:
The assessment identified the SCADA/PLC environment, water-treatment and distribution system, chemical-dosing controls, billing systems and customer information as particularly important assets. 
Compromise of OT systems could affect the physical operation of the water utility, while compromise of the billing and customer systems could result in data exposure and disruption of business services. 

Security Considerations: 
The asset inventory provides the foundation for the subsequent risk assessment. 
Asset criticality was considered when evaluating the potential impact of threats such as ransomware, supply-chain compromise, unauthorised remote access, false telemetry and unsafe manipulation of industrial processes. 

Key Takeaway: 
AMW's environment contains both IT and OT assets, meaning cybersecurity controls must protect information and business systems while also considering the safety, availability and operational requirements of industrial infrastructure.
