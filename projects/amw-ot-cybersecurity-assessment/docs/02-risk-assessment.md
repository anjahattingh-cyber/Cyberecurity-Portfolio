Risk Assessment

Overview: 
An ISO 31000-based risk assessment was used to identify, analyse and evaluate cybersecurity risks affecting Amanzi Metro Water (AMW). 
The assessment considered the likelihood and potential impact of threats across both IT and OT environments. 

Risk Categories
The assessment considered risks including: 
- Supply-chain compromise
- IT-to-OT lateral movement
- Ransomware
- Unsafe chemical dosing
- False or manipulated telemetry
- Compromised contractor credentials
- Customer data exposure
- Regulatory and compliance risks

Risk Assessment Approach
Risks were evaluated using a likelihood and impact approach. The resulting risk level was used to determine which risks required greater attention and stronger security controls. 
The assessment considered the potential effects on: 
- Water-treatment and distribution operations
- Safety and physical processes
- Customer information
- Business operations
- Availability of critical services
- Regulatory and compliance requirements

Key Risks
| Risk | Potential Impact | Key Security Consideration | 
| --- | --- | ---| 
| Supply-chain compromise | Compromise of trusted systems or software | Third-party security controls and monitoring |
| IT-to-OT lateral movement | Unauthorised access to industrial systems | Network segmentation and Industrial DMZ | 
| Ransomware | Loss of system availability and operational disruption | EDR, backups and incident response |
| Unsafe chemical dosing | Potential disruption to water-treatment processes | OT monitoring and access controls | 
| False telemetry | Incorrect operational information | Monitoring and validation of OT data | 
| Compromised contractor credentials | Unauthorised remote access | MFA, least privilege and controlled remote access | 
| Customer data exposure | Loss of sensitive customer information | IAM, monitoring and data protection | 

Risk Treatment
The assessment linked identified risks to appropriate security controls, including: 
- Network segmentation
- Multi-factor authentication
- Role-based access control
- Privileged access management
- Endpoint detection and response
- SIEM and security monitoring
- Incident response procedures
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Third-party risk management
- Secure remote access

Key Takeaway: 
The risk assessment provides a foundation for prioritising security controls across AMW's IT and OT environments. Risks affecting critical operational systems require consideration of both cybersecurity and operational consequences.
