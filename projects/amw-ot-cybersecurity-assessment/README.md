Amanzi Metro Water: Cybersecurity Assessment

Overview:

This project examines the cybersecurity risks and security requirements of Amanzi Metro Water (AMW), a simulated South African state-owned water utility operating critical IT and Operational Technology (OT) infrastructure. 

The assessment focuses on protecting water-treatment and distribution operations, SCADA and PLC systems, customer and billing information, remote contractor access, cloud services, and third-party suppliers. 

Project Objectives: 
The project applies cybersecurity and risk-management frameworks to the AMW environment, including: 

- ISO 31000
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CFS) 2.0
- CIA Triad
- Purdue Enterprise Reference Architecture
- NIST SP 800-61
- MITRE ATT&CK and ATT&CK for ICS
- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Continuity of Operations Planning (COOP)
- IEC 62443
